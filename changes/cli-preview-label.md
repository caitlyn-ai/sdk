- **In preview, and says so:** the CLI ships on the stable channel, with the SDKs, but its commands, flags and output
  may still change. Its docs, `caitlyn version` (and `stability` in `caitlyn version --json`), the top-level help
  and the installers say so; other commands' output and errors don't. See [The Caitlyn CLI](/cli/).
